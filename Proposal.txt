Introduction:
For my project I have chosen an online bookstore management system. Traditional bookstores
often rely on manual systems for managing books, customers, and sales. This can lead to
inefficiency in inventory tracking, difficulty in managing orders, and limited accessibility for
customers who must visit the store physically. The purpose of the Online Bookstore System is to
provide a web-based platform that allows customers to browse and purchase books online
while enabling administrators to manage book inventory, authors, categories, and customer
orders efficiently through a structured relational database.

System functionality:
The system will perform operations which are role defined. Customers can register/log-in,
browse books, purchase books and view order history (more features might be added as I build
the project). The administrator will manage all the tables like books, categories, orders etc.

System Users:
There will be two kinds of users, customers who use the system and the administrator who will
manage the system.

Technologies Used:
On the front end HTML, CSS and JavaScript will be used (Bootstrap might also be used). On the
back end Python will be used and for the database MySql will be used.
